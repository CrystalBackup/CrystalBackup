Soak collector: ON. It runs the SAME image as the operator above, keeps five streams on a

Check on day one AND day two that it is really collecting — this is the cheapest defence
against finding out on day fourteen that nothing was:

  kubectl -n {{ include "crystal-backup.namespace" . }} logs deploy/{{ include "crystal-backup.fullname" . }}-soak | grep soak-heartbeat
  kubectl -n {{ include "crystal-backup.namespace" . }} exec deploy/{{ include "crystal-backup.fullname" . }}-soak -- /manager soak-export --status

One heartbeat line per day, each naming what it collected. A day with no line is a day
with no data, and it is visible at a glance rather than after an export.

Then collect with hack/soak/collect.sh. The protocol is hack/soak/README.md.
